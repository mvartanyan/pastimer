# Working on Pastimer

Read [README.md](README.md) and [docs/handoff.md](docs/handoff.md) when starting a new session. The handoff records implemented work, validation, remaining deployment work and the owner's decisions. Update it when those facts change.

## Project decisions

- Use Rust for the backend (Axum, Tokio, SQLx) and SQLite for persistence. Node.js is development tooling for browser tests, not a backend runtime.
- Build common multiplayer, persistence, deck, audit, scoring and UI facilities for Telegram card games. The first game is the owner's variant of Thousand / тысяча.
- Treat [English rules](docs/thousand-rules.md) and [Russian rules](docs/thousand-rules.ru.md) as the agreed specification. Keep both synchronized if rules change; ask about genuine new ambiguities rather than silently adopting another Thousand variant. Keep the rules files about the rules.
- Treat [Durak English rules](docs/durak-rules.md) and [Russian rules](docs/durak-rules.ru.md) as the agreed specification for both Durak variants. Keep them synchronized. Durak helpers request approval per player and rank; a new rank needs a fresh request and approval. Use a fixed-height, two-row scrolling hand for Durak, without the ten-slot limit or an expanded overview. Keep Durak cards at least 76px wide on compact screens and 84px normally; use 44px minimum exposed hit areas, make the full face of an unpaired card tappable, and preserve readable controls instead of shrinking the table to fit.
- Thousand publishes the shuffled deck hash and deals immediately; players do not acknowledge it. The legacy high-card demo still uses acknowledgements.
- Place played cards by their owners, keep the trick leader highlighted, and visibly highlight the winner. Animate the three cards into the winning hand during the end of the existing three-second closure window, with a static reduced-motion alternative. After three seconds a completed trick joins its winner's pile. Players can inspect the most recent trick won by **each player**, including themselves, throughout the current deal.
- Normal play fits the Mini App viewport. Cards play on one tap; eligible marriages are selected by default when leading a legal king/queen. Keep the voluntary opt-out before playing. Show the current trump at the top centre with two equal circular gold rings viewed from above and the suit. Put large rings on the announcing card. Show the contract only under its owner's hand/seat. Inspect last won tricks from the seat/hand display, without a separate pile row.
- Auction calls are paced three seconds apart and shown in seat speech bubbles. Keep each player's latest call until the auction ends, then show the contractor and contract. The log retains every call.
- Drag stock transfers from the hand onto the recipient seat/hand, with touch and mouse support. Tap a staged card to turn it face up (open) or face down (secret); drag it back to cancel. Keep one atomic Give action on the table and no separate lower transfer panel. Staged cards leave the visible hand/count until returned or committed. Recipients see open cards face up and secret cards face down; reveal a secret card, then tap again to sort it into the hand. Use the incoming card highlight without visible sender/tap instructions, and suppress all pending receipt prompts once trick play begins, including before the recipient's first card. Pending receipts must never disable legal cards or pause forced play in the trick phase. Hand counts come from the actual server hand. Taken stock disappears from the table.
- Computer policy receives only its own typed view and public played cards, never the room or other hands. Bids combine guaranteed points with a modest estimate of likely tricks and one attainable marriage; recalculate the final order after stock distribution. Preserve winning leads/marriages when distributing and playing. Card plays wait one second; auction calls retain three seconds.
- Keep the table, hand and action-row footprint stable across turns, empty hands and deal endings. Keep ten pale hand slots; played cards leave placeholders. Show the full current-deal points (captured cards plus marriages and any dealer bonus) in one sum during a deal, cumulative scores in Record/between deals, and an icon for the barrel. Marriage opt-out belongs in the fixed hand options or the deliberate long-press/right-click card menu, without a lower bonus row. Marriage rings on played cards have a transparent background and partial opacity.
- Keep the complete interface localized through the persistent English/Russian/German/Spanish switch, without mixed-language promotional copy. Provide direct solo buttons for two or three computers.
- A single legal human card plays automatically after two seconds while the table is visible and unobscured. Preserve its deadline across local repaints; pause for inspection or incoming-card handling, and cancel on leaving/changing state. Revalidate the live view and use the normal durable command path.
- Tapping an in-progress archive entry resumes the table directly; expose Record separately. Rejoining an existing member through the existing join endpoint also remembers that table for server-side recovery.
- Played-games history and records are member-only, read-only projections. Include participants, scores, start/end times, public event hashes and deck commitments; unfinished private cards/proofs stay sealed. Reading history, records or audits must never advance a game.
- Table recovery must work with fresh browser storage after Telegram re-authentication. Preserve server-side membership lookup and actor-scoped durable command IDs.
- Preserve hidden information in public views and events. Reveal a settled or cancelled deal's full deck/movements/tricks in its audit, while keeping an unfinished deal private. See [the trust model](docs/architecture.md) for what the commitment actually establishes.

## Development

- Browser work is headless-only unless the owner explicitly asks for a visible browser in the current task. Verify `headless: true` before launching; do not attach to shared browser tabs.
- Assets in `public/` are embedded in the Rust binary via `include_str!`; rebuild and restart after frontend edits. There is no frontend bundler.
- Local development: `DEV_AUTH=1 cargo run --locked`, then open `http://127.0.0.1:3000`. Use separate browser profiles for players. `.env.example` is a reference; the app does not automatically load `.env`.
- In the managed cloud only, `. scripts/rust-env.sh` activates its workspace-local Rust toolchain. On a local computer use its normal Rust installation and the pinned `rust-toolchain.toml`.
- Check Rust changes with `cargo fmt --check`, `cargo clippy --all-targets --locked -- -D warnings`, and `cargo test --locked`. For UI/protocol changes also run `npm run test:browser` after installing the optional Node/Playwright dependencies.
- Browser suites launch isolated servers and temporary databases. `node scripts/capture-screenshots.mjs` captures real game flows using signed synthetic Telegram identities; it does not validate a live Telegram client. See [the screenshot gallery](docs/screenshots/README.md).

## Persistence and deployment

- Preserve existing local work and databases. `.data/`, SQLite/WAL files, `.env`, build output and credentials do not belong in Git. Take consistent backups before database/deployment changes.
- Keep command version checks, durable retry IDs, atomic snapshot/event/receipt transactions, explicit public DTOs and persisted trick deadlines intact. Preserve supported snapshot/audit compatibility.
- Horse is the owner's configured SSH server. Its established configuration is in the separate local **k3s horse** project; inspect that project and local SSH/kube configuration before choosing deployment settings.
- Horse is deployed at https://1000.vartanyan.com with @pastimerbot. Use the resolved `deploy/horse/` overlay and [the current release record](docs/horse-clarity-2026-11-16.md). Follow [docs/deployment.md](docs/deployment.md), verify the target cluster and live image, use one SQLite writer, and preserve its PVC. The generic `deploy/k3s/` ingress remains a template.
