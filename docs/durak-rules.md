# Durak - Pastimer's agreed rules

[Русская версия](durak-rules.ru.md)

Choose **Podkidnoy Durak (подкидной)** or **Perevodnoy Durak (переводной)** when creating a table. Both use the shared rules below. Perevodnoy additionally allows transfers as described in its own section.

A **game** runs from the initial deal to a loser or a draw. An **attack** includes the opening cards, all throw-ins and any transfers, ending when the table is discarded or picked up. The **original attacker** opens that attack; the **current attacker** controls attacking priority and may change after a transfer.

## Cards, players and objective

- Two to six players, each playing individually; there are no teams.
- Thirty-six cards: A, K, Q, J, 10, 9, 8, 7, 6 in each suit.
- Rank strength, highest first: A > K > Q > J > 10 > 9 > 8 > 7 > 6. Cards have no point values.
- Play proceeds clockwise. Players who have finished the game are skipped.
- The objective is to get rid of all cards after the stock is exhausted. The last player holding cards loses and is the durak. There is no single overall winner among the other players.

## Deal, stock and trump

- The dealer deals six cards to each player, face down, one at a time clockwise.
- With two to five players, the next card is exposed and placed beneath the remaining face-down stock. Its suit is trump for the whole game. Its rank and suit stay visible, and it is the last card drawn from the stock.
- With six players, all 36 cards are dealt. The dealer's last card is shown to everyone to determine trump, then remains in the dealer's hand. There is no stock.
- The trump six cannot be exchanged for the exposed trump card.

## First attacker and rematches

- In the first game, the player holding the lowest trump among the dealt hands attacks first. They may lead any card; they do not have to lead that trump.
- If nobody was dealt a trump, the player immediately after the dealer clockwise attacks first.
- In a rematch after a loss, the player immediately before the previous loser clockwise attacks first, so the first attack is against that loser.
- After a draw, determine the first attacker by the first-game rule again, including the no-trump fallback.

## Opening and extending an attack

- The attacker attacks the next active player clockwise, who becomes the defender.
- The opening may contain one card or several cards of the same rank.
- Every additional attacking card must match a rank already on the table. Both attacking cards and cards used in defence supply matching ranks, including cards already covered.
- An attack contains at most six attacking cards, including the very first attack of the game. Defence cards do not count toward this limit.
- The limit is also capped by the number of cards the defender held when becoming the defender for this attack. Beating cards does not reduce this fixed limit; picking up does not increase it. Transfers apply the new defender's limit as described below.

## Attacker priority and throwing in

- The current attacker has priority to add legal cards. Every other player except the defender may help, including at five- and six-player tables, but must first request permission using the "I want to throw in" button ("Хочу подкинуть" in Russian).
- The current attacker sees which players have pending requests and may approve a request or ignore it. A request alone never authorizes a play, reserves an attack slot or prevents the attack from ending; there is no automatic approval.
- Each request and approval applies to the requesting player and the rank they want to add. Approval permits that player to add legal cards of that rank for the rest of the attack against that defender; a separate approval for each card of the same rank is not required.
- Each newly introduced rank requires a fresh request and explicit approval before a helper may add cards of that rank. Approval for an earlier rank does not extend to the new one.
- Approved helpers receive opportunities clockwise from the current attacker, skipping the defender and players without approval for the cards they want to add. A player may add approved legal cards or pass; playing faster does not override this order.
- If a defence card introduces a rank not previously on the table, the current attacker gets first choice of the newly available plays. Helpers must request and receive approval for that new rank before continuing with it in clockwise order.
- Passing does not prevent a player from requesting permission for a newly introduced rank when their opportunity comes again.
- The defender cannot end a fully covered attack before the current attacker and any approved helpers have finished their opportunities. Ignored requests do not create an extra opportunity or require a rejection. If the current attacker does not approve helpers, only that attacker's decision to stop is needed.
- Requests and approvals end with the attack. A transfer clears them against the previous defender; helpers must request permission from the new current attacker again.

**Priority example:** A attacks B with a six, and B covers it with a nine. A has first choice to add sixes or nines. C presses "I want to throw in" to request permission to add nines. A sees C's request and may approve or ignore it. If approved, C may add nines at their clockwise opportunity; D needs their own approval. If B later covers a card with a jack that introduces a new rank, A gets first choice to add jacks. C must press the button again for jacks and receive a fresh approval, even if A already approved C's nines. Approval for nines remains valid for nines during this attack.

## Defending and picking up

- A non-trump card is beaten by a higher card of the same suit or by any trump. A trump is beaten only by a higher trump.
- Each defence card covers one attacking card. The defender chooses which uncovered card to beat and with which legal card. They may use a trump even if they hold the attacked suit.
- The defender may choose to pick up at any time, even if a legal defence is available, by declaring "Take" ("беру"). This commits them to picking up; they cannot resume defending or transfer the attack.
- After "Take", attackers may still add matching cards, with the same priority, permission, clockwise order and fixed attack limit. The defender then takes every card on the table, including defence cards and covered attacking cards.
- If every attacking card is covered and no further cards are added after the required opportunities, the defence succeeds. All table cards go face down to the discard and do not return to play. Reaching the attack limit leaves no further opportunity to add cards, but any uncovered cards still have to be beaten or picked up.
- After successful defence, the defender attacks next. After picking up, the defender misses their attacking turn and the next active player clockwise attacks. Skip players who have finished.

## Drawing and finishing

- After the attack ends, replenish hands to six from the stock before the next attack. A player already holding six or more draws nothing. Each player takes all cards needed before the next player draws.
- Start with the original attacker, then continue clockwise, leaving the final defender until last. If the original attacker became the final defender, begin with the next player clockwise and still leave the defender until last.
- If the stock runs out, later players receive only what remains or nothing. The exposed trump is drawn last.
- Running out of cards while the stock remains does not finish a player; they must replenish normally.
- Once the stock is empty, a player with no cards at the end of the attack and replenishment is finished and takes no further turns. An empty hand during an unresolved attack does not prematurely decide its result.
- Continue until just one player holds cards; that player loses. If the final two players both finish during the same successful defence, the game is a draw.

## Perevodnoy: transferring an attack

- In Podkidnoy, the defender must defend or pick up. In Perevodnoy, they may instead transfer before any attacking card has been beaten and before declaring "Take".
- To transfer, the defender plays a card of the same rank as all attacking cards currently on the table. The card joins the attack, and the entire attack moves to the next active player clockwise.
- The transferor becomes the current attacker and controls priority and throw-in requests against the new defender. Earlier requests and approvals are cleared; helpers must send fresh requests, which the new current attacker may approve or ignore for each requesting player and rank.
- The new defender must hold at least as many cards as the full attack now contains, including the transfer card. The attack must still contain no more than six attacking cards. Fix the new limit at the smaller of six and the new defender's hand size when receiving the attack.
- Transfers are allowed in the first attack of the game and may chain, including back to an earlier attacker, as long as each transfer is legal. In a two-player game this can transfer the attack back to its original attacker.
- Once any card has been beaten, no part of that attack may be transferred, including later throw-ins.
- Every transfer card is actually played. Showing a matching trump and keeping it ("проездной") is not allowed.
- Transfers change the current attacker but never the original attacker for drawing order. The final defender's success or pickup determines the next attack under the shared rules.

**Transfer example:** In clockwise order A, B, C, D, A attacks B with a six. B adds another six and transfers to C, becoming the current attacker. C may add a third six and transfer to D, becoming the new current attacker, provided D has at least three cards. D now faces all three sixes. If D has only two cards, C's transfer is illegal.

**Drawing example:** After that attack, A draws first, then B, then C, and D last, each up to six as the stock allows. If D instead legally adds the fourth six and transfers back to A, A is the final defender: drawing starts with B, then C and D, with A last. C or D becoming current attacker never moves them ahead of the original drawing order.
